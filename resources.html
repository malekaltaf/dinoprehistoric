Acoustic Bio-Resonance Laboratory

Experience synthesized bioacoustic simulations of dinosaur vocalizations and prehistoric soundscapes based on cranial resonating crests and syrinx models.

Infrasonic Bioacoustic
T-Rex Sub-Bass Rumble

Low-frequency closed-mouth infrasound (15–40 Hz) capable of traveling miles through dense Cretaceous forests.

Cranial Resonance Tube
Parasaurolophus Horn Call

Acoustic frequency modeled from 3D CT scans of the hollow, trombone-like cranial nasal crest passages.

Avian Pre-Syrinx
Raptor Communication Chirp

High-frequency coordination whistles and clicks analogous to modern ratites and predatory birds of prey.

Seismic Tremor
Sauropod Seismic Impact

Heavy ground vibration impact generated by 40-tonne sauropods displacing wet alluvial mud.

Aerial Vocalization
Pterosaur Coastal Scream

Piercing high-pitch screech echoing across ancient Tethys Ocean sea cliffs and lagoons.

Jurassic Rainforest Ambience
