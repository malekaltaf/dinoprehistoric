Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from visitors, educators, and paleontology enthusiasts.

Due to the weight and fragility of multi-ton mineralized petrified bones, complete standing mounts are typically museum-grade research casts molded directly from holotype fossil specimens. Real fossilized teeth, claw elements, and gastroliths are exhibited in controlled display vitrines.

Yes! We provide structured educational tours tailored to K-12 and university biology curricula. These include mock dig-pit excavations, fossil casting activities, and Mesozoic stratigraphy lectures. Please submit your group reservation request at least two weeks in advance.

You can submit high-resolution photographs with accurate measurement scales and locality details via our contact form. Our paleontology advisory team can provide educational preliminary assessments to determine if the specimen warrants formal academic examination.
