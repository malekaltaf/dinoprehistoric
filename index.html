The Age of Dinosaurs

Dinosaurs (*Dinosauria*) were the dominant terrestrial vertebrates for over 160 million years. Discover their scientific classification, evolutionary branches, and ancient fossil records.

Scientific Classification

Taxonomic Hierarchy (Owen, 1842)
Kingdom:
Animalia (Animals)
Phylum:
Chordata (Vertebrates)
Clade:
Dracohors & Archosauria
Superorder:
Dinosauria (Sir Richard Owen)
Temporal Range:
Middle Triassic – Present (Avian)

While non-avian dinosaurs became extinct 66 million years ago during the Cretaceous–Paleogene extinction event, modern birds (*Aves*) are surviving feathered theropod dinosaurs.

The Three Mesozoic Periods

The tripartite division of the Mesozoic Era spanning 180 million years

252 – 201 MYA

Triassic Period

Emerging from the Permian mass extinction, the supercontinent of Pangaea gave birth to the earliest primitive dinosaurs like *Coelophysis* and *Herrerasaurus*.

Read Era Details
201 – 145 MYA

Jurassic Period

Lush greenhouse jungles flourished. Gigantic sauropods shook the continents, plated stegosaurs roamed, and early feathered ancestors took to the skies.

Read Era Details
145 – 66 MYA

Cretaceous Period

The pinnacle of dinosaur diversity and size, culminating with apex predators like *T. rex*, armored ankylosaurs, and the fateful Chicxulub asteroid impact.

Read Era Details
